# Purdue Solar Racing Steering Assembly

![Purdue Solar Racing steering assembly](./path-to-your-image.png)

*CAD rendering (side view) of the steering hub, coupling, and triangulated support I modeled for the vehicle’s front end.*

## Description
I modeled the steering hub and the triangulated support bracket used to mount the steering column to the vehicle frame. The assembly features a removable wheel hub, a bearing/coupling housing, and a lightweight truss-style support designed to carry steering loads while minimizing weight.

My contributions:
- Modeled the hub, spacer, and coupling interfaces for serviceability and fastener access.
- Designed the triangulated support arms with internal cutouts to reduce mass while maintaining stiffness.
- Specified bolt patterns and mounting tabs compatible with the vehicle subframe.

## Features
- Removable steering wheel hub with spacer and bolted mounting plate
- Bearing/coupling housing with serviceable clamps and through-bolts
- Triangulated support arms with lightening cutouts and bolted pivot attachments
- Base flange for attachment to a vertical column/post with pin retention

## Technologies & Skills
- CAD modeling (assembly & part modeling)
- Mechanical design for manufacturability and serviceability
- Fastener selection and interface definition
- Lightweight structural design (triangulation and cutouts)
